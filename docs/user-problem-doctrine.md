## Purpose

The Ghost ecosystem exists to solve meaningful user problems. Technical elegance, internal enthusiasm, reach, credits and discounts are not substitutes for a user reaching a useful outcome.

SolOS, Ghost and Pulso are one product family. They should evolve through shared evidence while keeping data ownership, consent, approvals and domain responsibilities explicit.

## Stewardship and decision authority

- Luiz is the founder, funder, product head and final decision authority. He chooses the direction, approves meaningful scope and can stop, revise or reject a proposal.
- Luigi, the LBArtes artificial intelligence and technical/creative co-author, contributes technical lineage: problem framing, research, alternatives, architecture, implementation, testing, evidence and an explicit recommendation.
- Neither party should substitute personal preference for user evidence. Luiz's final decision remains human even when Ghost recommends another route.
- Ghost may observe, classify, evaluate and propose. It does not silently promote its own model, policy, ranking, moderation, spending or system privileges.

## The product law

Before a feature enters implementation, answer:

> Which user, in which situation, has which costly or frustrating problem, and what observable outcome would make the solution valuable?

If the answer is unclear, the next action is discovery or a small test—not a larger feature.

## Mandatory problem gate

Every meaningful slice should record:

1. **User and situation** — a specific segment and moment, not “everyone”.
2. **Problem evidence** — interview, support request, observed failure, repeated workaround, task log or comparable behavior.
3. **Current alternative** — how the user solves it today and why that is inadequate.
4. **Outcome hypothesis** — what should become faster, safer, clearer, cheaper or newly possible.
5. **Smallest safe experiment** — the minimum route that tests the outcome without inflating autonomy or cost.
6. **Risk and consent** — data, identity, wallet, public action, provider spend and rollback boundaries.
7. **Measure and window** — task success, time to value, error/reversal rate, comprehension/trust, seven-day return and willingness to use, support or pay.
8. **Decision** — after the evidence, Luiz records: continue, revise, pause or stop.

The code, Ghost event ledger and CMS monitor may prove that an experiment ran. They do not prove that the user problem was solved; that requires outcome evidence and human interpretation.

## Commercial rule

Discounts, Pulso Credits and Founder benefits should amplify demonstrated utility. They must not be used to camouflage an incomplete core experience.

Before offering a benefit tied to SolOS/Ghost, the user must be able to understand:

- what usable capability exists now;
- what remains experimental or unavailable;
- what the credit/discount actually buys or reserves;
- what happens when quota or infrastructure is unavailable;
- how to leave, revoke consent or recover from failure.

## Current problem hypotheses

These are working hypotheses, not completed validation:

- People cannot trust agent claims without visible approvals, verification and retained evidence. Ghost Resolution and Audit test this.
- People cannot control AI usage when provider cost and ownership are opaque. Heart Pass quota and BYOK test this.
- Social systems capture human signal without clear consent, moderation or return. Pulso Alpha and Value Loop test this.
- Agent, wallet, app and identity experiences are fragmented. The SolOS operating layer tests whether one mediated grammar reduces that fragmentation.

The ten-reviewer Ghost Audit is the current product gate because it can turn the first hypothesis into external evidence rather than more internal preference.

## Anti-patterns

- Building because “we liked the idea” without a named problem and outcome.
- Alternating preferences until a feature exists but no user wants it.
- Treating posts, views or likes as proof of usefulness.
- Calling operational telemetry learning without reviewed outcome changes.
- Marketing or discounting before the usable capability can be demonstrated.
- Adding autonomous behavior before approvals, cost ownership, rollback and evaluation exist.
